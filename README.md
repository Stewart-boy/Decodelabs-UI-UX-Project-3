Verdant Harvest: Premium Organic Grocery Experience

A high-fidelity UI design project focused on building a consistent visual identity for Verdant Harvest, a premium organic grocery delivery concept.

The project explores how colour, typography, imagery, spacing, and interface elements can work together to communicate freshness, trust, quality, and a premium brand experience.

Completed as Project 3 during my DecodeLabs UI/UX Design Internship Program, this project focuses on translating a visual direction into a cohesive digital experience across key product screens.

Introduces the Verdant Harvest brand through a minimal layout, leaf-inspired identity, and refined typography.

🎯 The Challenge

The goal was to create a premium organic grocery shopping experience that communicates freshness, trust, and quality without compromising usability.

The design needed to:

* Establish a recognisable visual identity.
* Make product categories and information easy to navigate.
* Present pricing, quantity, and purchase actions clearly.
* Maintain visual consistency across the splash screen, product list, and product details.

🎨 Visual Direction

The visual system combines natural colours, refined typography, high-quality product imagery, and intentional spacing to establish a consistent brand experience.

Colour Palette

* Leaf Green — represents freshness, growth, and nature.
* Deep Leaf — provides contrast, emphasis, and visual stability.
* Harvest Gold — adds warmth and premium accents.
* Earth Brown — introduces a natural, grounded feel.
* Cream — creates a calm background and allows content to breathe.
* Charcoal — supports readability while maintaining a softer appearance than pure black.
* Error Red — reserved for system feedback and error states.

Typography

* Playfair Display: Used for headings and brand elements to establish an elegant, premium tone.
* Montserrat: Used for body text, labels, prices, buttons, and other interface elements to support readability.

The combination balances editorial sophistication with practical, everyday usability.

🧠 Design Approach

The design was guided by four principles:

* Trust: Use relevant product imagery, clear information, and consistent interface patterns.
* Freshness: Apply natural colours and imagery that reflect the organic grocery concept.
* Clarity: Maintain a clear visual hierarchy across product information, pricing, and actions.
* Consistency: Apply the same typography, colours, spacing, and styling across all key screens.

📱 Key Screens

Splash Screen

Introduces the brand using a leaf-inspired mark, refined typography, and a minimal composition.

Product List

Supports product discovery through category tabs, search, product cards, pricing, organic labels, and accessible add-to-cart actions. Bottom navigation provides access to Home, Categories, Shop, Orders, and Profile.

Product Details

Focuses on purchase decisions through prominent product imagery, a freshness badge, delivery information, product descriptions, quantity controls, and a clear checkout action.

🔄 Design Process

Brief → Visual Exploration → Style Tile → High-Fidelity UI → Consistency Review

The process began with the visual foundation before moving into the individual screens.

The Style Tile established the colour palette, typography, and spacing direction. These decisions were then applied across the Splash Screen, Product List, and Product Details to maintain a consistent visual identity.

💡 Key Takeaway

Visual identity is more than choosing colours and fonts. It helps establish the character of a product and shapes how users perceive and interact with it.

This project strengthened my understanding of how to build a visual system and apply it consistently across high-fidelity interfaces while keeping usability, readability, and product information clear.
